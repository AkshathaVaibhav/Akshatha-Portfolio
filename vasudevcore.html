THE EXPERIENCE

Learning by building.

During my Web Development Internship at VasudevCore, I gained practical experience in frontend development by working on a real-world website development project. As part of the internship, I contributed to developing the website for MES Convent and English High School, Thavarekere, focusing on presenting institutional information through a structured and responsive web interface.

The project gave me an opportunity to apply web development fundamentals to a live institutional website and understand the process of taking a website from development to deployment.
